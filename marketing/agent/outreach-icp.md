## Who we look for

- **Industry and energy companies, mid-size: 50–500 employees.** Check the size on the LinkedIn company page or the imprint. Outside the range = no lead.
- **Owner-led or CEO-led**, where the CEO still takes part in daily processes. Signs: founder or family name in the company name or management, one or two managing directors, the CEO named as contact for sales or service.
- **Person to contact, in this order:** CEO / Geschäftsführer / Inhaber / Managing Director / owner; if not reachable: COO, head of operations, kaufmännischer Leiter, head of sales or service.
- **Not a target:** clinics, medical and dental practices, hospitals; corporations and their subsidiaries with more than 500 people; public authorities; one-person firms.

## What counts as a finding

One fact anyone can verify on the company's own website or careers page, about how work reaches them or is handled:

- quotes or enquiries only through a general form, a PDF form or one info@ address;
- service, maintenance or spare-parts requests only by phone, email or fax, no portal or ticket;
- open back-office jobs that show manual work (Sachbearbeiter Auftragsabwicklung, Innendienst, Disposition, order processing, data entry);
- product or technical documentation only as PDF downloads, no search;
- several sites or business units behind one contact inbox.

Save the page URL. No finding = no lead. Never guess internal facts (which ERP they use, how many requests they get).
