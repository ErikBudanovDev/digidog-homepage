**Post (EN, hook 1)**

Every ad account looked fine.
Together they were paying twice for the same clicks.

We connected Claude to a tour operator's Google Ads, GA4 and booking data: one custom MCP server, 51 tools, every account under the manager account at once.

In the single-account view, every number looked normal.

Side by side, a second account was running the same campaigns again.
Duplicate spend: €18,099.

No one missed it out of carelessness. Comparing accounts line by line is work nobody has time for. For an agent it takes seconds.

Same setup, ordinary Tuesday: "How many bookings did the Berlin walking tour have in the last 10 days?"
Answer: 76 bookings, 148 guests, split by sales channel. No report built.

What I took from it:
→ Google's own MCP servers answer "what happened in Google".
→ The real question is "did the money come back". That needs ads joined with bookings, CRM or sales.
→ Keep the agent read-only on money. It finds and recommends, a person approves budgets and bids.

How many ad accounts does your team run, and when did someone last compare them?

Full case and setup steps in the first comment.
